About

A cosmetics counter at the Trade Fair.

Shalom Cosmetics & Beauty Concept is a beauty and cosmetics business based at the A.P.T Trade Fair Complex on Badagry Expressway, Lagos. We specialise in beauty products, makeup essentials and general merchandise.

Our shelves cover lip oils, lip glosses and lipsticks alongside makeup products and the accessories that go with them. We work with distributors for our makeup items and lip products, and we sell both to individual customers and to buyers purchasing in quantity.

Customers reach us two ways: in person at Shop 17, or on WhatsApp, where we confirm what is in stock, agree a price and arrange pickup or delivery.
